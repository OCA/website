Nothing to configure. Once installed:

- The company address of a portal user is displayed but cannot be edited, in the
  customer area as well as during checkout.
- The "Company Name" field is read-only for a contact whose commercial entity is
  its parent company, and a submitted value is ignored.
- A portal user saving their own address no longer changes the company address.

Internal users are not affected: editing a contact address in the backend still
propagates to the parent company as usual.
